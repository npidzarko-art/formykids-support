For My Kids

A private custody journal that's ready when you need it

For My Kids is an iPhone app for one parent. Write down exchanges, messages, incidents and expenses as they happen, track parenting time on a calendar, and turn it all into a clear, dated PDF report for your lawyer, mediator or the court.

Private by design. No account and no server. Your records stay on your iPhone. Your co-parent is never invited and never notified.

What it does

  • Log exchanges and visitation: on time, late, no-show, cancelled or refused
  • Record messages, incidents, health and school events, and your own involvement
  • Set your regular pickup and drop-off times once, then confirm each one with a tap: on time, late or didn't happen
  • Attach photos, screenshots, PDFs and other files to any entry
  • Track overnights against your schedule and see your share by month and year
  • Track shared expenses and what has been paid back
  • Create a numbered PDF report or a spreadsheet for any date range
  • Lock the app with Face ID, and save a backup file whenever you like
