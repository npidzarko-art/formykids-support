Common questions

Where are my records stored?

Only on your iPhone, inside For My Kids. They are included in your iPhone's iCloud or computer backup. There is no For My Kids account or server.

How do I move to a new iPhone?

If you restore the new iPhone from a backup of the old one, your records come with it. To be safe, first go to Settings > Save a backup file in For My Kids and save the file to iCloud Drive or Files. On the new iPhone choose Settings > Restore from a backup file.

Can my co-parent see what I write?

No. For My Kids is for one person. Nothing is shared unless you send a report or file yourself.

Can I change an entry after saving it?

Yes. Reports show when each entry was first recorded and note changes made more than 15 minutes later, with the time and what changed. That history is what makes a log credible.

What happens if my subscription ends?

You can still open For My Kids, read everything, create reports and backups, and delete entries. To add or change entries, start a plan again.

How do I cancel or get a refund?

Cancel in Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions on your iPhone. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I have a code. Where do I enter it?

In For My Kids, open Settings > Redeem code, or tap "Redeem code" on the plans screen.

Is this legal advice?

No. For My Kids helps you keep records. Ask your attorney how to use them in your case.
