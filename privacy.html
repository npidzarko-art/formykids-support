For My Kids

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

The short version: For My Kids does not collect your data. Everything you write stays on your iPhone. There are no accounts, no servers, no analytics and no ads.

What For My Kids stores, and where

The entries, names, dates, amounts, schedule and photos you add to For My Kids are saved in the app's private storage on your iPhone. They are not sent to the developer or to anyone else. The developer cannot see, read or recover your records.

If you use iCloud Backup or back up your iPhone to a computer, Apple includes For My Kids's data in that backup under your own Apple Account, the same as your other apps. That is governed by Apple's privacy policy, not this one.

Information we collect

None. For My Kids has no sign-in, sends no usage data, and contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking code.

Permissions

  • Camera: only when you choose "Take a photo" for an entry. The photo is saved inside For My Kids on your iPhone.
  • Photos: you pick the photos to add through Apple's photo picker. For My Kids only receives the ones you pick.
  • Location: only when you tap "Use current location" on an entry. The address is saved in that entry on your iPhone. For My Kids does not track your location.
  • Notifications: only if you turn on the daily reminder. Reminders are scheduled on your iPhone.
  • Face ID: only if you turn on App Lock. Face ID is handled by iOS; For My Kids never receives your face data.

Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are handled by Apple through your Apple Account. For My Kids learns only whether a purchase is active. We do not receive your name, email or payment details.

Sharing and exporting

Reports, spreadsheets and backup files leave your iPhone only when you choose to share or save them. Once you send a file to someone, we have no control over it. Backup files are not encrypted, so keep them somewhere safe.

Deleting your data

You can delete any entry in the app. Deleting For My Kids from your iPhone removes all of its data from the device. Copies in your own device backups are removed according to Apple's backup settings.

Children

For My Kids is intended for adults. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
